About me

From individual casework to stronger services.

My work brings together frontline child protection, legal analysis and practical learning. In Cox’s Bazar, I have supported children and families affected by displacement, coordinated with community and humanitarian actors, and helped teams deliver safer, more consistent services.

Today, I focus on capacity development: turning programme standards into useful training, coaching and tools that field teams can apply in their daily work.
